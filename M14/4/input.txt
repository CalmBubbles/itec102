Offline development is a major emphasis of this study. The application can be distributed on a self-hosted website or on free app markets like itch.io. The application's acceptability and performance as an educational tool for learners and educators was evaluated by the researchers using survey questionnaires. All devices, apart from iOS and macOS systems, may access the software. This study did not currently attempt to distribute the software on paid app markets such as the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store. This study did not focus on the market and mainly focused on the actual application. The subject of the conduct of this study were the students of Sta. Maria Integrated High School, Academic Year 2025-2026.
